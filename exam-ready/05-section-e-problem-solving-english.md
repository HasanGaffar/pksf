## Formula sheet (★★★ — mukhosto)
- % change = (new − old) / old × 100 · Successive change: `a%` then `b%` → net = a + b + ab/100
- Profit% = (SP − CP)/CP × 100 · SP = CP × (1 ± p/100) · Loss: CP = SP / (1 − loss%)
- SI = P·R·T/100 · CI amount = P(1 + R/100)^n
- Work: rate = 1/days; together = 1/(1/a + 1/b) = ab/(a+b) · Man-days: M₁D₁ = M₂D₂
- Speed = Distance/Time; avg speed (equal distance) = 2ab/(a+b); km/h → m/s: × 5/18
- Ratio a:b sharing T → a/(a+b)·T · Mixture: weighted average
- Probability = favourable/total · nCr = n!/(r!(n−r)!) · arrangements of n distinct = n!
- Square series n², cube n³; AP: next = +d; GP: next = ×r

## Part 1 — Quantitative aptitude (30)

### ★★★ Percentage, profit, interest
1. 15% of 640 = **96** (640 × 0.15).
2. A price of 800 is raised by 25%, then reduced by 20%. Final price? 800×1.25 = 1000; 1000×0.8 = **800** (net change 0%: 25 − 20 − 25×20/100 = 0).
3. Cost 500, profit 30% → selling price **650**.
4. Selling price 720 at a 10% loss → cost = 720/0.9 = **800**.
5. Marked price 2000; discounts of 15% then 10% → 2000×0.85×0.90 = **1530**.
6. Simple interest on 12,000 at 5% for 3 years = **1,800**.
7. Compound interest on 8,000 at 10% for 2 years: amount 8000×1.21 = 9,680 → CI = **1,680**.
8. Population 20,000 grows 10% per year for 2 years → 20000×1.21 = **24,200**.
9. Ratio: divide 160 in 5:3 → **100 and 60**.
10. Average of 5 numbers is 24; a sixth number 36 is added → (120+36)/6 = **26**.

### ★★★ Time, work, speed
11. A finishes in 12 days, B in 24 days. Together: 1/12 + 1/24 = 3/24 = 1/8 → **8 days**.
12. Pipe A fills a tank in 6 h, B in 12 h. Together: 1/6 + 1/12 = 1/4 → **4 h**.
13. 6 men finish in 8 days. 4 men take: 6×8/4 = **12 days**.
14. A train 120 m long at 54 km/h passes a pole: 54 km/h = 15 m/s → 120/15 = **8 s**.
15. 60 km/h for 2 h, then 40 km/h for 3 h: total 120 + 120 = 240 km in 5 h → **48 km/h**.
16. Round trip at 40 km/h and 60 km/h (same distance): 2·40·60/(40+60) = **48 km/h**.
17. A server handles 200 requests/second. In 15 minutes: 200 × 900 = **180,000**.

### ★★ Mixtures, ages, algebra
18. 4 L of 25% solution + 6 L of 10% solution → (1 + 0.6)/10 = **16%**.
19. Father is 3× son's age. After 10 years he is 2× son's age. 3s + 10 = 2(s + 10) → s = 10: **son 10, father 30**.
20. Solve 3x − 7 = 11 → **x = 6**.
21. Solve x² − 5x + 6 = 0 → (x−2)(x−3) = 0 → **x = 2 or 3**.
22. Rectangle perimeter 30, length 9 → width 6 → area **54**.
23. LCM and HCF of 12 and 18: **LCM 36, HCF 6**.

### ★★ Series, probability, counting
24. 2, 5, 10, 17, 26, ? → n² + 1 → **37**.
25. 3, 6, 12, 24, ? → ×2 → **48**.
26. 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, ? → Fibonacci → **13**.
27. P(Ace) from a 52-card deck = 4/52 = **1/13**.
28. Two dice, sum = 7: 6 outcomes / 36 = **1/6**.
29. Arrangements of the letters of CODE: 4! = **24**.
30. Choose 3 people from 5: 5C3 = **10**.

## Part 2 — Logical reasoning (15)

### ★★★
1. Coding: CAT → DBU (+1 each letter). So **DATA → EBUB**.
2. Walk 5 km north, 3 km east, 5 km south. Distance from start: **3 km (east)**.
3. Pointing to a man, Rina says, "His mother is my mother's only daughter." The man is Rina's **son**. (Mother's only daughter = Rina herself.)
4. All developers are engineers. Some engineers are managers. "Some developers are managers" — **does not necessarily follow**.
5. A is taller than B, B taller than C, D shorter than C → **A tallest, D shortest** (A > B > C > D).
6. All A are B, and no B is C → **no A is C**.
7. In a class of 40, Rahim is 12th from the top → **29th** from the bottom (40 − 12 + 1).

### ★★
8. Odd one out: Java, Python, C++, Oracle → **Oracle** (database; others are languages).
9. Analogy: Doctor : Hospital :: Teacher : **School**.
10. Letter series: A, C, F, J, O, ? → gaps +2, +3, +4, +5, +6 → **U**.
11. Square numbers 4, 9, 16, 25, ? → **36**.
12. If 21 Nov 2026 is a Saturday, 1 Dec 2026 is a **Tuesday** (+10 days).
13. Clock: angle at 3:00 = **90°**.

### ★
14. 4 machines make 4 units in 4 minutes. 8 machines make 8 units in **4 minutes**.
15. Syllogism approach: draw Venn circles; a conclusion is valid only if it holds in **every** possible diagram.

## Part 3 — English grammar & vocabulary (30)

### ★★★ Grammar (answer in bold)
1. She ___ to work every day. → **goes**
2. They have lived here ___ 2020. → **since**
3. Neither of the answers ___ correct. → **is**
4. He ___ not know the answer. (do/does) → **does**
5. The meeting starts ___ 10 a.m. → **at**
6. He is ___ honest engineer. → **an**
7. If I had known, I ___ earlier. → **would have come**
8. Each of the files ___ checked. → **was**
9. Passive: "The team completed the report." → **The report was completed by the team.**
10. Indirect: He said, "I am busy." → **He said that he was busy.**
11. Correct: "She don't like tea." → **She doesn't like tea.**
12. Correct: "I am agree with you." → **I agree with you.**
13. Choose: "The data ___ stored securely." (is/are) → **is** (acceptable in technical writing; *are* also used formally).
14. Choose: "He is good ___ mathematics." → **at**
15. Choose: "She has been working here ___ five years." → **for**

### ★★ Vocabulary
16. Synonym of *reliable* → **dependable**
17. Synonym of *mitigate* → **reduce/alleviate**
18. Antonym of *transparent* → **opaque**
19. Antonym of *scarce* → **abundant**
20. Synonym of *scalable* (software) → **able to handle growth**
21. One word: "a person who writes computer programs" → **programmer**
22. One word: "a place where data is stored" → **repository / database**
23. Correct spelling: accomodation / **accommodation**
24. Correct spelling: recieve / **receive**
25. Idiom "to cut corners" → **to do something cheaply or carelessly to save time/money**

### ★ Sentence correction
26. "He gave me an useful advice." → **He gave me a useful piece of advice.** (advice is uncountable; *useful* begins with a /j/ sound → *a*)
27. "Each of the engineers have submitted." → **Each of the engineers has submitted.**
28. "The informations are correct." → **The information is correct.**
29. "I look forward to meet you." → **I look forward to meeting you.**
30. "Despite of the delay, we finished." → **Despite the delay, we finished.**

## Part 4 — Short writing (templates) (★★★)

**Rules:** short sentences · formal tone · one idea per paragraph · greeting + purpose + details + closing · re-read for spelling.

### A. Email requesting clarification
**Subject:** Clarification Required on Monthly Reporting Requirements

Dear [Name],

I am working on the monthly reporting module and need clarification on a few points: (1) the reporting period and cut-off date, (2) mandatory fields and validation rules, and (3) user access levels for partner organizations.

Your confirmation will help us finalize the API contract and database design on schedule. Please let me know a convenient time to discuss.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

### B. Incident update (ongoing issue)
"Our reporting service is currently experiencing higher-than-normal response times. The engineering team is investigating database latency and recent deployments. Users may notice delays when generating reports. We are applying safe mitigations and will share another update within 30 minutes or as soon as the cause is confirmed." *(Facts confirmed vs. under investigation — alada rakho; cause age bole dio na.)*

### C. Explain a technical problem to a non-technical stakeholder
"The report is slow because the system is processing a very large amount of data at once. We are optimizing how the data is retrieved so reports load faster, without reducing accuracy or changing who can see the data."

### D. Paragraph: Importance of data security
"Data security protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information. A secure system uses strong authentication, server-side authorization, encryption in transit and at rest, least-privilege access, input validation, audit logging and tested backups. For an organization handling partner data, a single breach can damage trust, so security must be built in from design to operation."

### E. Paragraph: Teamwork
"Effective teamwork depends on clear communication, shared goals and accountability. Team members should document decisions, review each other's work, raise risks early and give constructive feedback. This improves quality, reduces rework and helps the team deliver reliable software on time."

### F. Application/cover paragraph (why you are suitable)
"I am a software engineer with experience in Java, Spring Boot, PostgreSQL and React. I have built secure REST APIs and data-driven web applications, and I am interested in contributing to PKSF's mission through reliable, well-designed systems. I am confident that my technical skills and commitment to quality will add value to your team."

## Quantitative aptitude

1. **Percentage:** 20% of 850 = **170**. Calculation: 850 × 0.20.
2. **Percentage increase:** 500 to 600 = **20%**. Increase 100/500 × 100.
3. **Ratio:** Divide 640 in ratio 3:5. **Answer: 240 and 400**.
4. **Average:** Average of 12, 15, 18, 25 = **17.5**.
5. **Simple interest:** Principal 10,000, rate 8%, time 2 years = **1,600**. SI = PRT/100.
6. **Compound amount:** 10,000 at 10% annually for 2 years = **12,100**.
7. **Work:** A completes in 10 days, B in 15 days. Together = **6 days**; rate 1/10 + 1/15 = 1/6.
8. **Speed:** 180 km in 3 hours = **60 km/h**.
9. **Profit:** Cost 800, selling 1,000. Profit percentage = **25%**.
10. **Discount:** 20% discount on 1,500 gives price **1,200**.
11. **Series:** 2, 6, 18, 54, ? → **162** (×3).
12. **Series:** 1, 4, 9, 16, ? → **25** (squares).
13. **Series:** 3, 8, 15, 24, ? → **35** (n²−1).
14. **Probability:** One fair die, probability of an even number = **1/2**.
15. **Probability:** Two coin tosses, probability of two heads = **1/4**.
16. **Work rate:** 6 workers finish in 12 days; 9 equally productive workers need **8 days**.
17. **Mixture:** 2 L at 10% plus 3 L at 20% gives **16%** concentration.
18. **Data rate:** 12,000 records in 4 minutes = 3,000/minute; in 15 minutes = **45,000** at constant rate.
19. **Fraction:** 3/4 + 2/3 = **17/12**.
20. **Equation:** 3x + 7 = 28 → **x = 7**.

## Logical reasoning

21. All APIs are software; some software is open source. Does it follow that some APIs are open source? **No**, not necessarily.
22. If A > B and B > C, then **A > C**.
23. Odd one out: PostgreSQL, MySQL, Redis, Spring Boot → **Spring Boot** (application framework).

25. Complete: 5, 10, 20, 40, ? → **80**.
26. A is north of B; C is east of B. From A, C is **south-east**.
27. If today is Tuesday, 17 days later is **Friday**.
28. Arrange ascending: 0.8, 2/3, 0.72 → **2/3, 0.72, 0.8**.
29. If 4 machines make 4 units in 4 minutes, 8 machines make 8 units in **4 minutes**.
30. Find the pair: 2:8, 3:27, 4:64, 5:? → **125** (cube).

## Short writing — model answers

### 46. Incident update: reporting API is slow
“Our reporting API is experiencing higher-than-normal response times. The engineering team is investigating database latency and reviewing recent deployment and traffic metrics. We are monitoring the impact and applying safe mitigations. We will share a further update when the cause and resolution are verified.”

### 47. Email requesting clarification on a requirement
**Subject: Clarification Required on Reporting Requirement**

Dear Team,

Could you please confirm the expected reporting period, mandatory fields, validation rules and user access levels for this report? This will help us finalize the API contract and database design.

Regards,  
[Your Name]

### 48. Explain a technical problem to a non-technical stakeholder
“The report is taking longer than expected because the system is processing a large amount of data. We are optimizing the database query and checking the underlying data. The goal is to improve response time without compromising accuracy or access controls.”

### 49. Short paragraph: importance of data security
“Data security is essential for maintaining confidentiality, integrity and availability. A secure system uses strong authentication, server-side authorization, encryption in transit, least-privilege access, input validation, audit logging and tested backups. Security should be considered throughout design, development, deployment and operation.”

### 50. Short paragraph: teamwork
“Effective teamwork depends on clear communication, shared goals and accountability. Team members should document decisions, review each other’s work, raise risks early and provide constructive feedback. This improves quality and helps the team deliver reliable software.”

## Exam writing checklist
- Use short, grammatically correct sentences.
- For calculations, show the formula and working.
- For logic, state the rule that produces the answer.
- For incident communication, separate confirmed facts from investigation.
- Avoid claiming a cause or resolution before evidence confirms it.
